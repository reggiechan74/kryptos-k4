# Study 061 method learning

## Retained validation capabilities

Duplicate detection compares actual 97-position maps, not cipher-family names. A linear column-chunk recognizer checks membership in every ragged column order without factorial enumeration; 192 positive controls cover natural/reversed column orders at widths 2–97. Exact map catalogs and independently implemented public-source membership comparators retain source hashes and descriptive counts separately from cryptanalysis counts. These are established mathematical/software verification techniques, not a claimed new cryptanalytic invention.

Permutation nonmembership rules out a subset relation regardless of the prior key domain. A matching permutation alone does not establish duplicate coverage: alphabets, key domains/indexing, arithmetic/stage order and completion/threshold status must also match. The width-97 columnar superset trivially contains every permutation, but no completed exhaustive width-97 keyed search was established; it therefore does not disqualify the study.

The all-zero key is a genuinely prior-covered subdomain through the pure-transposition E-count contradiction. By contrast, statistical IC arguments and an invalidly generalized direct-position Bean argument do not exclude every nonzero constant key under arbitrary transposition. The study preserves these distinctions rather than upgrading broad third-party claims to proofs.

The independent cylinder reference and exhaustive tiny forward oracle validate exact free-key representation. The solver never reports a selected completion as an identified plaintext or sums overlapping nominal-period cylinders as a union cardinality. The public mask observes every residue for periods 1–8; sparse controls deliberately test the ambiguity that this mask would otherwise conceal.

## Change gate

Retain the exact algebraic solver unless measured evidence establishes a bottleneck. A future optimization must identify a real cost and pass fresh independent held-out controls without changing these frozen rules. A negative cannot justify new checksums, width schedules, directions, phase shifts or added layers. New mechanism proposals require a separate prior-art/analytical-subdomain gate.

## Measured benchmark cost and decision

The sealed 416-case production batch used about 1.229 inclusive CPU seconds; the independent reference used about 1.583. Exact free-cylinder/candidate analysis used 3.488 seconds for 79,865 roundtrip probes. There is no cryptanalytic search bottleneck to justify a neural scorer or stochastic optimizer. Prior-route auditing involved millions of inexpensive membership checks; that preparation is separately accounted, not advertised as millions of new K4 cipher trials. Retain the algebraic method and the stricter prior-domain auditing workflow.
