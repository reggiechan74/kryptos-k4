## Coordinate convention and key-domain requirement

Use q[t]=i (output position t receives input position i), or inverse m[i]=t consistently. Sum-locked rotation specializes to `C[m[i]]=A[(rank_A(P[i])+K[i mod p]) mod26]`, A=AZ or KR, p1–8. Josephus specializes to `C[t]=A[(rank_A(P[q[t]])+K[t mod p]) mod26]`: its key is OUTPUT-indexed, not plaintext-indexed. Matching a permutation alone is insufficient. Both inverses were included in the route comparison to prevent convention errors. Per-description key periods can alias; do not count a repeated-period extension as a new key function.

With 24 clue positions and p<=8, the usual collision redundancy 24-number_of_observed_residues is at least16 regardless of route. Thus Scott's retained-hit thresholds4/5 and zeyeteam's fixed-alphabet threshold6 cannot omit these cells, unlike their longer-period domains. The arbitrary-alphabet equality threshold is separate and should not substitute for the fixed-alphabet branch.
