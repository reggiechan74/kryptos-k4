## What this establishes

- Exact exclusions are conditional on declared symbols, framing, alignment and operators. One failed pilot does not close a family.
- Counterfactual edit freedom guarantees some first-clue fits and defeats a count-only exclusion without identifying a message. The final arm retains that ambiguity rather than turning it into a promising plaintext.
- Synthetic solver success is not K4 recovery; timeouts/resource limits are unresolved, not negatives.
- Source context is not a complete algorithm. Physical measurements, historical inventories and independently selected encodings remain concrete missing inputs.
- The public English clues are constraints at stated positions. Altered streams need explicit mappings; they are not historically untouched validation data.
