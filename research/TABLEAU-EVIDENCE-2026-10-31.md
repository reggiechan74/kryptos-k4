The [Smithsonian collection record](https://collections.si.edu/search/detail/edanmdm%3AAAADCD_coll_22298) corroborates the collection, **not item-level authentication of these JPEGs**. No institutional image identifier, full-resolution original capture, or item-level box/folder citation was established here. The dates, authorship and purpose of the individual markings remain unknown.

These circles are on a full cyclic lookup tableau, not the separate [2010 K1/K2 worksheets](https://kryptools.com.about.ag/Sheets/sheets.htm). Scott Dunphy's [pinned chart-letter-key campaign](https://github.com/scott-dunphy/kryptos-k4/blob/3ce19e3ae164a54af8743222ad3de5f6a93f6faf/NOTES.md) already explores marked letters as keys/alphabets. That does not prove coverage of these exact coordinates, but generic marked-letter-key enumeration is not a new strategy.

No evidence fixes a K4 role, extraction order, key interpretation or coordinate-to-cipher rule. **Do not queue a cipher test from these marks.** A future admission requires independently grounded K4 semantics and a resolved extraction/provenance packet, not unconstrained enumeration. This update resolves the earlier missing-public-pixels limitation while preserving the broader evidence gate and external history.
