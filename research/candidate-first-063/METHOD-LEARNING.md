# Study 063 method learning

## Retained exact-search capability

Local crib constraints can cheaply derive complete periodic-key seeds without enumerating26^p keys, but independently resetting the hidden state at each crib is only a relaxation. A concrete tiny counterexample is retained: ciphertext `AAAAA`, alphabetABC, high modulus2, period1, cluesAA atpositions0–1 andAB at3–4. The two local key sets intersect atkey2, yet no message-start state yields a globally valid plaintext. Both implementations and exhaustive forward enumeration reject it. This fixture makes global reachability a tested requirement, not just prose.

The independent scalar-state implementation and explicit low/high-state production offer different representations of the same normal form. Exact tiny forward truth checks filters and complete global tuples, including old-high readout and carries 0/1/2. Parameter aliases are retained rather than conflated with unique plaintext identification.

## More precise prior-work reasoning

The prior geometric-radix family has nonlinear glyph features, so a generic nonlinearity claim would not clear it. The retained 104 state-pair witnesses use the precise prior even-glyph parity invariant, checked independently with 2,704 next-key extensions and reachability of every hidden state. Fixed-alphabet causal-affine families need a separate conditional-translation argument. Set-valued adjacent carry noise is not silently treated as a deterministic linear recurrence. These distinctions prevent broad source labels from turning into unsupported exclusion claims.

The proposed machine is a scalar accumulator modulo 624 with a nonlinear digit readout, not a globally new gear primitive. Zero keys remain included because their transformation is still stateful, unlike prior pure-transposition cases.

## Calibration-driven budget choice

A single synthetic speed example underestimated the variable runtime: a twelve-case calibration found mean 0.1344 and maximum 0.4306 CPU seconds, with 25–450 compatible parameterizations. Before freeze, the design selected 688 fresh whole-union cases instead of 2,560, while preserving separate direct controls for all 7,488 alphabet/period/start-state combinations. The fresh holdout covers every initial state and exactly 26 cases in each alphabet/period/uniform-literal cell. No revealed holdout was reused and no correctness/null gate was weakened.

## Future gate

Only a measured bottleneck or a separately eligible new mechanism justifies another solver. Preserve the frozen equations, old-high timing and whole-message reachability. A future optimization must reproduce complete tiny and fresh held-out sets, not merely find the planted key. No post-negative resets, readout timing changes, added signs/routes or new modulus searches are warranted by this finite experiment alone.

## Observed identifiability limitation

All 624 fresh plants were included, yet every one had multiple compatible plaintexts. A post-reveal diagnostic of the sealed output files—no new solver run—found 8–126 distinct plaintexts per plant. Each plant had a same-key alternative differing only at position 0. Thus this model/crib regime supports exact compatibility testing or bounded exclusion, not demonstrated unique recovery. No English scorer should conceal that distinction.

One basic mechanism is that, when several valid(a 0,x 0) pairs share the same integer sum with fixedh 0/key, they emit the same first ciphertext and enter exactly the same next state; later clues cannot distinguish those first-letter alternatives. This explains an ambiguity source without claiming it is the only possible alias or that the observed counts are universal.

Measured production/reference benchmark CPU was about 11.367/39.743 seconds; full returned-candidate analysis used 3.493 seconds. There is no search-speed bottleneck. The more important retained lesson is to audit identifiability independently of correctness and avoid converting many exact compatible parameterizations into a solution claim.

The finer post-reveal projection separates first-letter freedom from other ambiguity:63 plants vary only at position0;424 additional plants vary only within positions0–20;137 also vary at later unknown positions. Across the624 cases,1–34 positions vary. Every plant's same-key first-symbol-only alternatives have exactly the same post-first hidden state, explicitly checked from the state sums. Per-case positions, nominal key groups, initial-state counts and pre-crib convergence remain in `analysis/ambiguity-projection.json`. These observations are outside the frozen admission gates and must not be generalized into a theorem about every possible key/message without proof.
