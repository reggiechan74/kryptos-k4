## Corrections and consequences

- Historical SIGABA was genuinely untested. The new [CSP-889 implementation and bounded result](SIGABA-CSP889-FINDINGS.md) now cover the authentic machine mechanism with a documented simulator wiring set and restricted starts. Twelve external known-answer vectors pass. The simulator's large-wheel wirings are not recovered secret wartime wirings; neither the custom predecessor nor this finite domain excludes all SIGABA.
- The four adjacent affine-digraph certificates covered equal alphabet roles only. The newly preregistered [four crossed-role structures](AFFINE-DIGRAPH-CROSSED-FINDINGS.md) also fail exact first-crib constraints. Mixed roles were not silently claimed covered by the older parity proof. General nonlinear blocks remain untested.
- H-PERM requires the literal unchanged 97-symbol A–Z model. Three required E letters cannot come from two ciphertext E letters by permutations alone. Substitution, insertion/deletion and changing representations invalidate that invariant's premises.
- H-PERIOD excludes exactly nineteen periods in 1–96 under unchanged alignment and one fixed bijection per phase. It does not exclude every period, routed arithmetic, arbitrary alphabets with separate input/output roles, or variable-length preprocessing. Necessary-check survivors are not cipher candidates.
- The autonomous order 2–6 and arbitrary-base affine-feedback results are explicitly **analytical exclusions of their stated structures**, not newly counted brute-force trials. Their current statuses now say so. The physical clock/lamp readout proof likewise excludes the fixed minute sampling/bit order only; the 11,520 labels were not independently searched discoveries.
- IPA's known English offsets are conditional phonetic-window placements, not established alignment through unknown prefixes. Greek phonetic tested all declared nonoverlapping transformed placements instead. Neither lossy transcription supplies a unique spelling inverse. The ordinary-alias IPA staging remains47 first-crib ordinary-anchor contradictions plus one shared-marker contradiction, not 48 ordinary-anchor first-stage proofs.
- Ten Gromark noncrib positions are forced **within that fixed primer/model**, not independently confirmed plaintext. Autokey's 420 underdetermined configurations and route fitting's 282 zero-check descriptions remain non-evidence; compatible free choices cannot be promoted by attractive English. Public Berlin checks are not historically untouched data.
- Alias accounting stays local: progressive replication is not new coverage; 16 old clock cases were skipped; phase/key-negation gauges are not separate evidence; raw settings, normalized schedules, full plaintexts and proven function equivalence are different counts. A planted selector recovering one plaintext on a fixture is not a universal unique-decoding theorem.
- The preserved 22 October strategy comparison had stale untested wording. A dated status note now links the current register instead of rewriting its historical plan. “No Greek converter” is narrowed to no **lamp-to-Greek** converter, since a Greek-phonetic experiment now exists.
- New [forwarded Smithsonian scan findings](PHYSICAL-EVIDENCE-PENDING-2026-10-23.md) distinguish an intended artificial pool lamp from sunlight and a static metaphorical compass needle from a moving magnetic instrument. This task has not independently inspected those scans. Full source addendum/visual replay is pending. Geometry conflicts remain named alternatives, not revised Site dimensions.
