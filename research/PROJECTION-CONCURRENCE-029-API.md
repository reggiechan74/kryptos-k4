# Projection concurrence: research/Site mathematical contract

Python reference: [implementation](../experiments/projection_concurrence.py), [independent scalar checks](../experiments/projection_concurrence_reference.py). Frozen [synthetic data and complete outputs](../results/PROJECTION-CONCURRENCE-029-SYNTHETIC.json) are the machine-readable handoff; `fixtures` contains eight named cases. This contract does not assert Site deployment.

Input: image `width,height` >0; `pairs=[{aperture:[x,y],receiver:[x,y],sigma_px:positiveNumber}]`; distinct zero-based `trainingIndices`. Both point arrays use the same original-image coordinate system; sigma is a declared isotropic per-coordinate pick uncertainty, not glyph size. Holdouts are all remaining pairs. No wrapping, mirroring, per-point crop offsets or angle tuning. Keep provenance and uncertainty outside synthetic widgets visibly separate.

1. Let scale=max(width,height). Normalize each point to `[(x-width/2)/scale,(y-height/2)/scale,1]`.
2. Each line is aperture cross receiver, divided by hypot(line[0],line[1]). Reject collapsed segments. Sum outer products of training lines to form a symmetric 3×3 scatter matrix.
3. Its smallest-eigenvalue unit eigenvector is the source in normalized homogeneous coordinates. Return all eigenvalues in increasing order. Relative eigen-gap `(lambda1-lambda0)/max(1e-12,lambda2)` below `1e-7` warns of nonunique/poorly conditioned source. Do not equate parallel distinct lines with coincidence: valid parallel lines meet at infinity.
4. Map source back to pixels as `[scale*sx+width*sw/2,scale*sy+height*sw/2,sw]`. Normalize homogeneous scale/sign for comparisons; equality is up to nonzero scale. A browser should not draw a giant finite point when `sw` is numerically zero. Show a direction indicator instead.
5. Predicted line for an aperture A is `A cross source`. Normalize first two coefficients and evaluate the receiving point to obtain signed pixel miss. If that line collapses, show an explicit undefined result. This is a **line prediction**, not a predicted 2D receiving point. Arbitrarily sliding a receiving point along that line retains zero miss.
6. For independent endpoint picks, propagate determinant `f=(ay-gy)*sx+(gx-ax)*sy+(ax*gy-ay*gx)*sw`. Gradient with respect to `[ax,ay,gx,gy]` is `[-sy+gy*sw,sx-gx*sw,sy-ay*sw,-sx+ax*sw]`. Normalize sigma by image scale. Divide f by the square root of summed squared gradient times sigma². This endpoint-only value omits fitted-source error/systematics.
7. Research output separately estimates fitted-source line uncertainty with 256 fixed-seed Gaussian perturbations of training picks and reports quadrature-combined heldout residuals. A live simplified display may show endpoint-only residuals **if explicitly labelled**; it must not claim numerical equality to combined research scores. Cross-language RNG differences can change perturbation summaries. Exact fixtures/source/eigen/residual comparisons do not require matching RNG.

The provisional synthetic line gate uses heldout combined RMS<=2 and max<=4, with no conditioning warning. It is not a monument acceptance claim or probability. Actual admission requires independently certified material-point pairs, camera assumptions and a predeclared finite-source/model-error budget. Near-concurrence cannot identify lamp versus Sun, a real versus reflected virtual source, historical time, north, metric depth, physical ray direction or K4 information.

Training indices `[0,1,3,4,6,7,9,10]`; heldout `[2,5,8,11]`. Primary teaching fixtures are `finite`, `heldout_mutation`, `coincident_lines`. Additional `infinity`, `distant_finite`, `noisy_finite`, `mixed_sources`, `collapsed` demonstrate limits. Positive exact finite source is `[120,-80]`; infinite image direction is `[1,0.3,0]`. Ground points/aperture points may be outside the synthetic display bounds in mathematical controls; do not clip their coordinates before analysis. Rescale/clip only visual drawing, preserve full underlying values.
