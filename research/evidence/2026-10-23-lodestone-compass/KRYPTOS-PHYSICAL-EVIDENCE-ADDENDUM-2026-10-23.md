# Kryptos physical-evidence addendum

Research date: 23 October 2026. Public archival records and photographs only. No outreach, purchases, repository writes, numerical bearing fit, or cipher test. This supplements the existing lodestone/compass dossier; it does not claim a K4 solution.

## Findings that change the evidence model

### 1. Artist-authored distinction between static needle and true north

Smithsonian Archives of American Art, Jim Sanborn papers, **Box 6 Folder 9**, undated book draft, digital image **AAA-AAA_sanbojim_4129077** (viewer page 7), describes a sandblasted compass dial and its **static** needle, which is “deflected metaphorically from true north” by the lodestone. This is stronger authorial evidence for the conceptual distinction than later solver diagrams. It supplies no numerical azimuth, magnetic reading, or surveyed alignment of the engraved N axis.

The same page describes entrance and courtyard **red-granite outcrops** as intended to share an axis and appear parallel from above. This must not be silently reassigned to the copper screen or interpreted as a surveyed compass-to-screen sightline.

- [Official folder record](https://www.aaa.si.edu/collections/jim-sanborn-papers-22298/series-3/box-6-folder-9)
- [Exact image 4129077](https://ids.si.edu/ids/iiif/AAA-AAA_sanbojim_4129077/full/full/0/default.jpg)

**Date and status:** Smithsonian dates the folder “undated.” The draft's introduction, image **4129058** (page 2), refers to the approaching twentieth anniversary, making circa 2009 plausible but inferred. It is a retrospective artist draft, not a signed 1989 construction specification or an as-built survey. [Introduction](https://ids.si.edu/ids/iiif/AAA-AAA_sanbojim_4129058/full/full/0/default.jpg).

### 2. Better-provenanced material and lettering dimensions, but no full screen plan

The same draft, image **AAA-AAA_sanbojim_4129085** (page 10), identifies Revere Copper Products and states **three-eighths-inch copper plate** and **two-inch-tall letters**, laid out in horizontal rows and cut with a hand-held jigsaw. These should enter the evidence ledger as retrospective artist specifications, not as present-day instrument measurements. They conflict with some secondary half-inch / three-inch descriptions; the conflict should remain explicit rather than be averaged away.

- [Exact image 4129085](https://ids.si.edu/ids/iiif/AAA-AAA_sanbojim_4129085/full/full/0/default.jpg)

No defensible full-screen width, height, local curvature, panel pose, or true-north registration was recovered. GSA's catalogue gives dimensions as “Varying,” which is an absence of useful metric detail, **not evidence that any particular published dimension is false**. [GSA catalogue](https://art.gsa.gov/artworks/23717/kryptos/).

### 3. Original maintenance instructions support a nighttime-light branch

**Box 6 Folder 10**, 1990 maintenance instructions submitted by Sanborn, images **AAA-AAA_sanbojim_4111603-000001** through **4111606-000001** (viewer pages 11–14), describe preservation of the appearance at completion. Page 14 specifies a **single 500-watt quartz lamp**: 17:00–24:00 fall/winter and 20:00–24:00 spring/summer. It also specifies small-pool pump hours, 08:00–20:00, April 1–December 1.

This establishes intended operating conditions for an artificial-light hypothesis. It does not establish actual historical uptime, lamp coordinates, beam profile, identical operation today, or a cryptographic role.

### 4. Genuine construction records found, with scope limits

The same folder contains James R. Urban, ASLA's **20 February 1990** letter about the CIA sculpture, image **4111616-000001** (page 24). It places entrance planting north of the entry walk beside the parking garage and south beside the cooling tower, and refers to six sketches. Sketch 4, image **4111621-000001** (page 29), depicts entrance landscaping and outcrops but supplies neither a north arrow nor a scale or compass detail.

- [Urban letter](https://ids.si.edu/ids/iiif/AAA-AAA_sanbojim_4111616-000001/full/full/0/default.jpg)
- [Entrance sketch 4](https://ids.si.edu/ids/iiif/AAA-AAA_sanbojim_4111621-000001/full/full/0/default.jpg)

An annular granite construction drawing, image **4111597-000001** (page 5), contains radii and fabrication dimensions. It does **not** identify the engraved compass circle or copper screen. Do not transfer its measurements to those objects. [Drawing](https://ids.si.edu/ids/iiif/AAA-AAA_sanbojim_4111597-000001/full/full/0/default.jpg).

### 5. Dimensioned later reproductions are not CIA as-built plans

**Box 18 Folder 12** contains tempting cutting diagrams, including 48 × 96-inch layouts, but also clear later-production context. Image **4871673** (page 32) is a 1992-dated English/Cyrillic panel request retransmitted by LAI in June 2005, showing a 72 × 30-inch drawing and an enlargement request. Image **4871680** (page 35), dated June 2005, is a **3.33 × 1.64-inch nickel-silver production layout**. These cannot establish original installed Kryptos dimensions.

## Historical World Clock: new finite photographic leads

Picture Alliance's catalogue supplies two identifiable **4 November 1989** views with the clock foreground and Hotel Stadt Berlin background:

- **16008401**, portrait, public preview 414 × 600: [image](https://img.picturealliance.sodatech.com/KARE/public/16008401.jpg).
- **16008415**, landscape, public preview 600 × 389: [image](https://img.picturealliance.sodatech.com/KARE/public/16008415.jpg).
- [World Clock catalogue](https://www.picture-alliance.com/en/webseries/berlin-world-clock-berlin-w194131)
- [4 November 1989 demonstration catalogue](https://www.picture-alliance.com/webseries/4-november-1989-berlin-alexanderplatz-demonstration-w363830)

Both previews were inspected. They show roof edges/panel boundaries and architectural context, but panel lettering is too small for reliable transcription. **No additional city names or panel azimuths are admitted from them.** Their value is a dated, concrete original-image lead, not an angle measured from a low-resolution oblique view. Neither image alone supplies the full circumference.

The Verein für die Geschichte Berlins photograph catalogue identifies additional potentially useful originals: **Mi-A245**, an easterly clock view, 1980s slide, 4912 × 3276; **Mi-A I615**, clock and hotel, 1976, 2512 × 3477; **Mi-A857**, southeast view, 1970s postcard, 4800 × 3428. The catalogue exposes metadata, not inspectable full images. These are pre-restoration leads, not proof of 1989 panels or unchanged orientation. [Catalogue page](https://www.diegeschichteberlins.de/geschichteberlins/datenbanken/digitales-fotoarchiv.html?start=650).

A Bundesarchiv search record for **Bild 183-1989-1104-437** was indexed as a 4 November demonstration photograph, but the live catalogue returned maintenance unavailability. Its equivalence to either Picture Alliance asset is **not established**. [Exact archival search](https://www.bild.bundesarchiv.de/dba/de/search/?query=Bild+183-1989-1104-437).

## Next evidence-backed avenues and stopping conditions

1. **Original photographic series, not another schematic.** Sanborn's draft image **4129149**, page 27, describes a post-completion 1990 courtyard photography session using a 4 × 5 view camera and 35 mm SLR, with roughly 200 exposures and about 150 slides. He says the entrance was not photographed then. This is a provenance lead, not confirmation that all originals survive. Smithsonian **Series 8, Box 11 Folder 34**, “Kryptos” photographs, is catalogued but has no public digitized-image link found in this audit. Stop online inference at that boundary; a later authorized archive inspection could determine format, sequence, capture dates and usable overlapping views. [Draft image 4129149](https://ids.si.edu/ids/iiif/AAA-AAA_sanbojim_4129149/full/full/0/default.jpg).
2. **1987–1988 sketchbooks.** Smithsonian **Series 7, Box 9 Folders 11–13** are concrete pre-construction candidates. The finding aid exposes metadata but no public image links. No contents or hidden images were inferred. An authorized future inspection should seek north arrows, dimensions, compass studies and installation revisions, distinguishing sketches from as-built records.
3. **World Clock originals and fabrication records.** Obtain sufficiently detailed dated originals of the two 1989 images before attempting label transcription or registration. The already documented Kunsch workshop construction-record/letter-template lead remains stronger than repeated generic web searches, but this pass did not obtain its actual plans or panel schedule. Access would require a new authorized archival/publication route; no contact or purchase was made.
4. **Photogrammetry gate.** A useful reconstruction requires dated overlapping views, original resolution, visible fixed control points, camera/lens estimation, surveyed map coordinates or independently verified building axes, and an uncertainty budget. Screen curvature requires a 3D rather than single-plane model. Compass rectification must distinguish engraved N, the static needle, defined stone points and true north. Clock registration must identify panel seams and labels in the same historical view, then verify the surrounding control geometry. Do not derive survey-like precision from preview pixels, an assumed circular shape, or top-of-image north.

## Coverage and conclusion

A contact-sheet audit visually screened **203 public scan pages** across Smithsonian Box 6 Folders 8–10, Box 3 Folder 6, and Box 18 Folder 12; selected relevant pages were examined at full resolution. This is not a transcription or exhaustive textual reading of all pages. Public IIIF manifests linked in official folder HTML exposed images even where the page's viewer status suggested processing was incomplete. Only public linked resources were used; no restricted or masked material was recovered.

**Still missing:** surveyed screen orientation/shape; survey-grade rose/needle/stone geometry; historical measured magnetic readings; complete 1989 World Clock panel schedule and geographical registration. These are unresolved inputs, not disproved hypotheses. The public folders inspected are a bounded exhausted branch; the specifically identified undigitized photographs/sketchbooks, historical image originals and fabrication records remain finite, evidence-backed avenues.
