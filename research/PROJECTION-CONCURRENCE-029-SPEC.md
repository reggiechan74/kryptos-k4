## Claim and identifiable quantity

For a central camera, the image of a straight source–aperture–receiver ray is a line. Correct pairs under an ideal single point source therefore have concurrent image lines, including projective points at infinity. This is necessary, not sufficient: concurrence does not identify pair correctness, depth, absolute scale, a unique three-dimensional source, time, north or a decoder. A distant Sun normally has a **finite image vanishing point**; a finite versus infinite image intersection does not classify solar versus lamp illumination. Camera-center/ray collinearity may collapse points and is degenerate. Unknown lens distortion or stitched/non-central imagery can invalidate straight-image-line assumptions.

Only **line incidence** is predicted for a withheld ground point from its aperture image and a fitted source image. Distance along that line and the full two-dimensional spot position are not identified without independently calibrated receiver/surface/camera geometry. Do not call line-incidence validation a complete optical projection prediction.

## Data gate fixed before annotation

Use a single original exposure with recorded catalogue identity, SHA256, dimensions and orientation/crop transform. No generative enhancement. Obtain at least 12 independently certified non-K4 point correspondences across at least three aperture rows and three horizontal regions, with 8 training and 4 spatially distributed held-out pairs fixed before fitting. Twelve is a practical design, not a universal sufficiency theorem; at least two distinct image lines mathematically determine a projective intersection, so two pairs alone provide no validation. Require at least three independent line directions overall; short segments, coincident/near-coincident lines and poorly conditioned arrangements can fail despite large counts.

Each pair needs aperture and receiving-feature pixel coordinates, literal point-identification rationale, transcription/row position establishing non-K4 status, source/crop linkage, and repeated blinded picks to estimate uncertainty. Geometric glyph centroids are not certified matching material points: projection, thickness and illumination can shift them differently. A readable letter identity alone does not establish a point correspondence. Freeze all pairs, fit/holdout assignment, endpoint uncertainties and independently motivated finite-source/model error allowance before fitting. No post-fit pair reassignment, removal, tolerance inflation or attractive-word selection. Retain rejected/ambiguous annotations separately.

## Frozen diagnostic and comparison

Normalize both image axes by the same known image scale (max(width,height)) about image center. Form each homogeneous line as aperture cross receiver, normalize its first two coefficients, and fit the minimum-eigenvalue unit vector of the training line scatter matrix. Use a standard-library symmetric 3x3 Jacobi eigensolver. Expose all eigenvalues and eigen-gap/geometry warnings; no automatic outlier removal or RANSAC selection. Report homogeneous source, finite coordinates when representable, per-pair signed perpendicular line miss in pixels, and delta-method incidence residual divided by propagated independent isotropic endpoint-pick uncertainty. The endpoint-only standardized residual is not a calibrated probability, and omits source-estimation/systematic error.

Separately quantify source-fit uncertainty by 256 deterministic endpoint-perturbation fits (seed 290024) using declared Gaussian pick errors; report per-holdout signed-miss standard deviation and quadrature combination with endpoint propagation. This is a conditional sensitivity diagnostic, not proof of Gaussian field errors or a confidence interval on a physical emitter. Repeat the analysis at 0.5,1,2 times declared pick uncertainty without changing pairs. An undeclared model-error budget makes real pass/fail **not evaluable**. Proposed line-only acceptance requires heldout combined normalized RMS <=2 and maximum <=4, plus nondegeneracy; report sensitivity instead of upgrading uncertain inputs to measured truth.

Independent reference: cross products/two-line intersections and a finite-coordinate 2x2 ordinary least-squares intersection. Compare exact/noiseless finite and infinity cases against the homogeneous fit; noisy estimates need not agree because objectives differ. Independently recompute determinant-gradient endpoint propagation by central finite differences. Explicitly test projective transforms and the failure of a unique full ground-point prediction.

## Pre-real-data mathematical amendment

Initial synthetic unit QA found the phrase “three independent line directions” overstrict: separated parallel lines can define a unique projective point at infinity. Require independent line **constraints** (rank two with a separated smallest eigenvalue), not three different directions. The initial near-parallel test incorrectly demanded rejection of a valid infinity configuration; its assertion is corrected to test near-coincident constraints. Original finite/infinity/noise/mutation fixtures and all numerical acceptance thresholds remain unchanged. No real data were fitted. This is a disclosed mathematical correction, not a relaxed real-data gate.
