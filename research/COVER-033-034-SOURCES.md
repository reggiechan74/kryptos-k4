# Word-cover sources, normalization and prior coverage

24 October 2026 UTC. [Source metadata and hashes](../results/COVER-033-034-SOURCES.json) preserve retrieval versus publication dates. Raw pages, corpus text and third-party software are not committed or executed.

- [NATO's official history and alphabet](https://www.nato.int/cps/en/natohq/declassified_136216.htm) supplies the 1956 standard, ALFA/JULIETT spellings and X-ray. The implementation removes the hyphen for the declared A–Z cover stream. The four custom dictionaries are adaptations selected from the user's known-word interpretation, not source evidence of Sanborn's method. Their exact words are exported in [data](../data/cover-codebooks-033.json).
- [Brown manual](https://listings.lib.msu.edu/public-corpora/cd421/manuals/brown/INDEX.HTM) and the [pinned official NLTK data file](https://github.com/nltk/nltk_data/blob/18fcb5421e3d590673282ee5c1624facf960b52c/packages/corpora/brown.zip) identify the finite corpus. ZIP SHA256 is `9b275f9b3b95d7bd66ccfb7cd259f445a13bbe5d1f4107aba09fd3e8364bafa6`. Its README states distribution with copyright-holder permission and permits redistribution; this study nevertheless publishes only hashes, source references and derived outputs. The 1961 print sampling, 1964 corpus documentation, later revisions and current digital packaging are distinct dates.
- [Official TaggedCorpusReader source](https://github.com/nltk/nltk/blob/ad9c96ba00a16923ffe917eacf63f1707bfa2d08/nltk/corpus/reader/tagged.py) confirms newline sentence tokenization in this data representation. It was read statically; no NLTK software installation or downloaded-code execution occurred. Our parser is separately implemented and validated.
- [Scientific American's firsthand event report](https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/cia-kryptos-puzzle-creator-releases-final-clues/) says the BERLINCLOCK clue refers to the Berlin World Clock. That is report attribution, not an independently read DocumentCloud scan. It is context against treating words as meaningless filler, not a proof against dual use or a new clock study.

## Prior scope

The existing IPA/ARPABET/SAMPA/dictionary-codec investigations address pronunciation or serialization before encryption. They do not test word-initial extraction after a full 97-letter decryption. [Word-interior scrambling](../docs/word-scrambling-hypothesis.md) also has a different stage and operator. [The August 2025 letter acrostic](../docs/august-2025-letter-acrostic.md) concerns that letter's paragraph markup, not this K4 cover grammar. Null/concealment systems and word acrostics are historical mechanisms; no claim of worldwide novelty is made.

The completed [spelling study](SPELLING-CORRECTION-032-FINDINGS.md) is separate and preserved. A post-decryption extraction does not incur its pre-encryption crib-length objection. The public English letters still occupy the original 97-letter stream; only a later readout is shorter. Unknown output word spaces and the conditional cover-token boundaries are not automatically the same.
