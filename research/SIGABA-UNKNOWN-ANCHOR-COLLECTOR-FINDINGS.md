## Mechanism and why it preserves keys

The first consecutive observed block is represented by exact current-cipher-state bitmaps. Under mask m, a source state u connects to u+step(m); implicit edge support is the intersection with a translated next layer. Forward/backward pruning retains connected support rather than pooling independent per-layer masks into a path. Equal collector digit sets must use equal masks for any fixed physical index function, so mask supports are intersected across all occurrences. At most eight monotone passes are permitted; an unfinished fixed point retains a weaker necessary superset. Time/memory interruption is unresolved, never empty.

The resulting controller-specific anchor restriction enters the exact COMPACT-018 recurrence. Cipher-anchor sets, offsets and static-map ownership remain correlated; unequal domains are not merged. Every physical map still executes continuously through prefix and gap. Map-dependent inverse translation reconstructs original starts. First-observation anchoring is a full-torus reparameterization, **not a reset**. Missing plaintext is not invented.

Support success is not a physical key: different paths and anchors can support different masks, and abstract mask choices need not correspond to one reachable map. Exact verification remains mandatory. The new layer combines connected support and repeated-collector restrictions; these runs do not isolate the causal contribution of repeats alone.
