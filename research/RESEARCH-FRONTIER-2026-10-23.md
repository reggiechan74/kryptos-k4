## Strongest gains from this session

- **Key-independent constraints beat arbitrary key samples.** [Wheatstone](WHEATSTONE-TRANSITION-FINDINGS.md) fails for arbitrary fixed ring permutations: ST at 24–25 yields RV, while ST at 33–34 yields SS. One fixed displacement cannot both change and preserve the inner pointer. [Fractionated Morse](FRACTIONATED-MORSE-ARBITRARY-FINDINGS.md) fails all 348,160 permitted first-crib word-break/stream-offset contexts, independently checked, without selecting a keyword or consulting Berlin. Both proofs have explicit preprocessing/alignment limits.
- **Unknown state need not be guessed.** The [fixed-tree follow-up](FEISTEL-TREE-EXTENSION-FINDINGS.md) grants every cipher-label permutation at each crib-island start. Each fixed primitive still contradicts the local observations. This rules out the same continuous primitives regardless of unknown prefix state; it does not test all trees or prove that arbitrary locally granted states are globally reachable.
- **More fit can mean less information.** Four three-round arbitrary-function structures have over 10^75 complete key descriptions each and different full plaintext witnesses matching all 24 cribs. Their exact counts are independently reproduced. They are non-identifiable, not decoding leads. The earlier Gromark tuple similarly has alternative full alphabet witnesses; its ten forced noncrib letters are model-conditional deductions, not newly observed plaintext.
- **The controller gap is algorithmic, not an exhausted machine.** Full cipher-start SIGABA inference covered its frozen 520 schedules, but full joint control/index inference remains unimplemented/budget-unresolved. The 66,058-class index quotient and centuries-scale naive projection are not a lower bound or proof that a better solver cannot work.
- **Archival discoveries constrain the model without supplying a key.** Artist records distinguish a static/metaphorical compass needle and intended granite-axis relationship; typed maintenance instructions place a timer-controlled quartz light in the small pool. They do not supply surveyed bearings, beam geometry or an extraction rule. Conflicting reported copper/letter dimensions remain separate accounts.

## English clues are not automatic intermediate-stream coordinates

The repository's working A–Z baseline places EASTNORTHEAST at final-English indices 22–34 and BERLINCLOCK at 64–74. A hypothetical preprocessing layer may change length, letter identities, word boundaries and framing. It needs an explicit map from final English to processed symbols and then ciphertext; it cannot simply reuse those integers in every intermediate stream.

The IPA 13/11 local length coincidence does not establish cumulative alignment through the unknown prefix/gap. Greek transcription had separate unknown-placement tests; it did not establish a universal reversible English encoder. Fractionated Morse used decoded-letter prefix length bounds and ternary offsets, rather than equating ciphertext indices with English indices. Wheatstone's certificate is conditional on the disclosed pairs being adjacent **processed** symbols aligned with the stated ciphertext positions; invisible spaces or untracked insertions are not silently granted or universally disproved. Roundtrips through a lossy preprocessing layer recover the processed string, not necessarily the original spelling.

Berlin is a public clue used throughout earlier research, not historically untouched data. A first-stage fit followed by a public second-stage check is useful staging, but fitted mappings, new component offsets or new symbol values are not independent predictions. Repeated EAST, parity relations and repeated-letter equations are consequences of the same 24 letters. The previous shuffled p≈0.0866 calibrates only its frozen 24 periodic models, not this entire research history or the rotor family.

## Runnable next steps without pretending missing inputs exist

From the repository root:

```sh
PYTHONPYCACHEPREFIX=/tmp/k4-pycache python3 experiments/frontier_assessment.py --output /tmp/K4-FRONTIER.json
```

This checks all 60 stable entries, gate references and available local artifacts, then reproduces the saved review. It performs **no network request, cipher fit, search launch or replay execution**. File presence is not historical authentication. Each gate's `safe_replay_argv` preserves an existing executable validation command and explicitly says what it does not establish. Those commands are replays, not newly approved cipher domains. `null` means the necessary inputs or runnable implementation do not exist; there is no fabricated command behind it.

A genuinely new observation, authenticated distinct procedure or sound solver implementation can reopen a specific gate. It must receive a new versioned protocol, independent controls and honest fitted-versus-predicted accounting before K4 evaluation. Until then, progress is blocked by those concrete information/algorithm gaps—not by lack of permission, and not by a claim that K4 or all cryptography has been exhausted.
