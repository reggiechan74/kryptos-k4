Evidence levels are explicit in each entry:
- **Documentation inspected:** a report or proposal was read; its execution/completeness may not be established.
- **Source/model inspected:** relevant implementation was read, not run or proven correct.
- **Named result inspected:** a particular committed output/receipt was read, not independently reproduced.
- **Independent reproduction:** none in this update. Do not silently promote any entry to this level.

“Reported exact” remains the source author's bounded claim. A timeout, skipped system, failed annealer, thresholded zero, one gibberish satisfying completion or unrun proposal is not a general exclusion. Distinguish descriptions, restarts and iterations from unique encryption operators.
