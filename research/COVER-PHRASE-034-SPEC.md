Retain all legal splits/counts; do not apply this as free recombination to manufacture a target phrase. Generate31 deterministic draws, seed340024. Per draw and per custom dictionary, sample one exact97letter cover uniformly over all compatible covers in the all-count stratum and, where nonempty, independently in the15-coverword stratum. Extract all12 fixed k/N/phase choices from the same sampled cover, preserving their correlation. For every output test Branch-A corpus-window membership and all Branch-B splits. Report each draw's maximum over all dictionaries/strata/selectors/branches, counts by lane, and full sampled synthetic inputs. Sampling does not make unknown K4 plaintext available.

The backward Branch-A bank query is a source-only feasibility operation independent of actual ciphertext. With the same length/crib-conditioned inputs, repeating that entire bank search under a ciphertext-null gives the identical result.
