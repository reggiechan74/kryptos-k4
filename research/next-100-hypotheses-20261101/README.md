## What “different” means here

Each record specifies an operator, finite model or measurable algorithmic claim; a closest known local/external comparison; a bounded test and controls; and an identifiability limit. Distinct mathematical definitions do **not** establish that nobody tried them on K4. Broad undocumented campaigns remain unresolved overlaps. Source-listed “proposed”, author-reported “tested” and independently reproduced results must remain separate.

Classical generators, compression codes, automata, group actions and optimization methods are existing mathematics. The prospective contribution is the precisely bounded application, composition or test—not an invention claim. Some mechanisms postdate 1990 or have no creator-use evidence; these are explicitly speculative mathematical tests, not historical deductions. A complicated model able to fit arbitrary plaintext is not progress.

The new mruckman1 dossier changes eligibility: generic cellular-automaton streams, unbounded homophonic selectors and generic number-sequence libraries cannot be advertised as new. Its exact source scopes still need comparison against applicable proposals. Decipher supplies methodological prior art: generic agent orchestration, calibration or candidate retention is not new either.

## Before any target execution

1. Read current project instructions and AGENTS/HYPOTHESES/EXTERNAL-PRIOR-ART/EXPERIMENTS again; check for work completed after this baseline.
2. Compare the **exact** equations, stage order, alphabet, state update, key/index convention, bounds and alignment against prior local and external code. Record witnesses of difference or equivalence. If unresolved, keep blocked; never turn missing source into novelty evidence.
3. Prove/check inverse and framing on independent tiny oracles. Reject degeneracies into excluded families. For learned/adaptive forms, constrain all initial state and parameters before observing K4.
4. Pre-register one finite synthetic protocol, positive plants, deliberately misspecified/null controls, expected outcomes, costs and stopping rules. Run that protocol only under the separate execution workflow. The budgets in these drafts are proposed caps, not measured feasibility or allocated compute.
5. Admit a surviving mechanism into the canonical queue with a frozen protocol. Do not “rescue” a failure by changing alphabets, keys, ordering or bounds without a genuinely new, separately justified proposal.
6. Report compatible/incompatible/incomplete at the tested scope. Preserve all equivalent keys and complete plaintext relations where relevant. A compatible decipherment and exact re-encryption alone do not prove intended plaintext.

The public 24 clue letters are constraints, not fresh held-out validation. Method experiments proposing blind islands use **new synthetic** data only. Physical speculation is not converted into a clue; no new physical/archive-derived mechanism was admitted in this pass.
