## Portfolio ordering

The machine-readable catalog includes a deterministic review rank for all 100 proposals. It is a **research triage**, not a claim of measured expected success: the first five above lead; remaining records sort by their recorded priority tier, then put method validation before mechanism exploration within a tie, then stable ID. Tie order has no scientific significance. Costs are proposed pilot caps, not timings measured on completed experiments.

- **High value now:** source/operator dedup, exactness and inverse/framing checks, then small independently specified models.
- **Later:** expensive structural models whose hidden state may destroy identifiability, and speculative coding/routing compositions lacking creator evidence.
- **Lowest confidence:** famous deterministic sequences and classical stream generators selected without independent evidence. These are explicitly alternative submodels of one scalar-keystream architecture, not seven independent clues or new cryptographic inventions.
- **Blocked specifications:** C05 (SEQUITUR repair/serialization), B15 (exact sequence convention), S12 (bit/rotation convention), and S15/S16/S33 (register/cycle conventions) are not executable until frozen. A08 requires a particularly strong prior-orbit alias check. Any additional per-record proof/source blocker takes precedence over rank.

A negative source/inverse gate retires the proposal without a K4 run. A useful result is often the decision **not** to search.
