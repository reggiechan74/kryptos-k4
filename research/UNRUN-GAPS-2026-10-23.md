prerequisites, not worldwide novelty or an exhaustive taxonomy of possible ciphers.

## Evidence-dependent pipelines, not ready new decoder trials

- **Historical World Clock to letters:** the [strategy comparison](STRATEGY-RESET-2026-10-22.md)
  and [historical audit](../independent/2026-10-22/historical-clock/historical_clock_report.txt)
  leave a complete 1989 inventory/global registration and an independently
  justified A–Z encoder unresolved. A partial mechanical coordinate operator
  was tested; a defensible clock-based K4 decoder was not. More abstract 24-column
  routes are already tested and are not this missing operator.
- **Surveyed optical decoding:** the [geometry audit](../docs/scott-sun-geometry-audit-2026-10-22.md)
  leaves metric surface shape, aperture contours, heading and receiver paths
  unsurveyed. Conditional photo scenarios and the Site are useful tools, not a
  calibrated optical K4 decoding test. This is an input gate, not an unconsidered
  theory or permission to infer a key from an attractive alignment.
- **A broader new synthesis grammar:** exact graph/CRT solvers, toy counting,
  restricted feedback grammars and autonomous recurrences have already been
  used. The [comparison](STRATEGY-RESET-2026-10-22.md) leaves a genuinely new,
  source-justified composite grammar undefined. Calling another solver SAT or
  adding fitted freedom would not be a new strategy.
- **Broad open-world architecture diagnosis:** a small closed-world classifier
  and control audit was run; a larger abstaining, independently sourced
  diagnostic remains deferred. Its current forced labels on uniform controls
  show why this is a methodological extension, not evidence about K4.

## Do not revive stale unrun labels

- The 72 arbitrary-primer mixed-alphabet configurations and 32 split-coordinate
  cases were completed in [CONTINUATION-FINDINGS.md](CONTINUATION-FINDINGS.md).
- The K3 route layers/sandwiches, 24-sector routes and carved-mask cases were
  completed; a route or period tweak is not an untouched family.
- The four fixed evolving alphabets and their specified reset extensions were
  tested. Restricted affine feedback and autonomous recurrence orders 2–6 were
  also excluded. Broader nonlinear/state models remain outside those scopes,
  but that alone does not specify a new experiment.
- The adjacent affine-digraph recipe is **analytically excluded**, not an open
  search awaiting implementation. The four [parity certificates](AFFINE-DIGRAPH-PARITY-BOUNDARY.md)
  supersede historical unrun headings without a matrix search.
- Fixed-alias IPA and the free-map periodic extension have local exact negative
  outcomes. ARPABET/SAMPA/AHD source feasibility is a separate incoming audit;
  a codec comparison is not an executed outer-key search or proof of full
  phonetic alignment. No additional codec model is launched by this note.

"Not excluded by a narrow test" and "never tried" are different statements.
These records support a small set of unrun proposals and unresolved input gates;
they do not establish a promising untouched cipher family or justify unbounded
searching. The current canonical ciphertext and public crib coordinates remain
unchanged.
