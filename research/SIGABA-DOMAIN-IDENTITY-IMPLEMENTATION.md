Dense-fixture `translate` has 2.488 seconds self of 4.093 total; it is not the selected target. This records an engineering choice informed by profiling, not a new historical hypothesis or blind cipher discovery.

Replace only large integer cipher-domain keys with immutable value objects storing the exact integer plus its hash computed once at construction. Equality compares the full exact integer and never treats matching hashes as equality. Equal sets have equal keys even when independently constructed; collisions must remain distinct. An unchanged intersection retains the same object. No pool of live domains is retained globally; reference lifetimes match live correlated branches. Owners, controller order, offsets, mask updates, cipher predicates, cache partitions, observations, candidate retention and resource caps are unchanged. Memory admission counts both wrappers and their exact integer payloads, preserving immutable sharing and reserved headroom.

The original sparse implementation remains frozen and its measured receipts are not rewritten. Shared trajectory DAGs and compressed cipher sets are explicitly NOT introduced in this batch: first remove the measured repeated-key cost.
