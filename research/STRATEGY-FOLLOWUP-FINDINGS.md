## Scope and discrimination controls

Exactly four initial pairs R/S0: AZ/AZ, AZ/KRYPTOS, KRYPTOS/AZ,
KRYPTOS/KRYPTOS. C_i=S_i[index_R(P_i)], followed by moving observed C_i to the
front, preserving other relative order. Inverse P_i=R[index_S_i(C_i)], then the
same update. Index 0 starts the full 97-letter message; no separator or resets.
All 24 crib letters are predictions conditional on the model, with zero fitted
symbols. Selecting among four models is still a choice informed by public clues;
it is not historically independent validation.

A reachable one-letter ciphertext prefix D makes each model's next-letter map
fall outside the entire mixed-role AZ/KRYPTOS affine superset: 208 role/sign/shift
descriptions, 207 distinct maps. This strictly contains the previous fixed-tableau
cursor domains, so this pilot is not another phase variant of those cases.
It does not establish novelty relative to all possible state machines.

Before K4, 32 synthetic 97-letter planted messages (8/model) retained the actual
EASTNORTHEAST and BERLINCLOCK strings/indices, including repeated-letter relations.
They all recovered fully and uniquely under a selector that received no generator
label. Independent rank-dictionary encoder/decoder, 104 one-step checks, state
permutation checks and 32 mutated-crib rejections passed. These are programmatically
blinded controls, not an external human blind or a natural-English corpus.

The same four-model selector rejected 32 nearby cyclic-rotation generators and
199 fresh histogram-preserving planted-cipher shuffles. Each decoy used the same
24 exact crib constraints. All 199 yielded no compatible model. This passes the
frozen small harness gate; it does not calibrate performance on every unknown
architecture. Decoys may be correlated and the corpus is deliberately conditioned.
No K4 partial-score significance is claimed; all four cases are already exactly
contradicted, so no additional K4 null simulation was necessary.

## Toy volume and prior sensitivity

The toy has alphabet ABCD, fixed length-12 schedule
`[0,1,3,2,1,0,2,3,0,3,1,2]`, and all 24 squared ordered alphabet pairs:
C_i=S[(index_R(P_i)+k_i) mod4]. Fit only positions 0,1,4,7, preserving the other
eight for non-fit predictions. Every candidate's direct inverse agrees with an
independent forward-table oracle. Coupled rotations give gauge orbits of four:
576 assignments reduce to 144 global function classes. Conditional raw survivors
range from 4 to 24 for planted cases; distinct compatible plaintexts range 1–6.
All forced non-fit symbols agree with planted truth. Counts, plaintext classes
and predictions remain consistent under gauge changes.

Twenty planted cases and their twenty matched shuffles were processed identically.
Fourteen shuffles are impossible and six compatible. The small prior assigning
mass only to four predeclared alphabet pairs is incompatible in 19/20 planted
cases, even though the full uniform prior contains their true keys. This
illustrates prior/model misspecification, not evidence for an artist's preference.
Raw assignments, gauge classes, distinct plaintexts and weighted predictive
counts are retained separately. No SAT timeout or larger synthesis claim follows.

## Independent recurrence proposal: overlap only, not rerun

The separately commissioned model K_i=a*K_(i-1)+b+d*C_(i-1) mod26,
C_i=P_i+K_i, has 26^4 times two alphabets = 913,952 parameter descriptions.
These are not all new models or distinct plaintexts. Known included domains:

- d=0,a=1 is period-1 progressive substitution, already covered by DYNAMIC-KEY-001.
- d=0,b=(1-a)*k makes the state stationary, hence constant-key substitution.
- (a,b,d)=(0,0,1) is plus CT-autokey lag 1; (25,0,1) is plus PT-autokey lag 1.
- (0,0,25) is minus CT-autokey lag 1 with negated effective primer;
  (1,0,25) is minus PT-autokey lag 1 with negated effective primer.

Those lag-1 cases are included in AUTOKEY-CONSTRAINT-001 with arbitrary primers.
This list is a proved subset of overlap, not a complete alias classification;
autonomous periodic states may overlap further. Fixed-slot 21-bit description
length is an encoding convention, not minimum complexity or a probability.
No recurrence search was duplicated here and no independent-worker result is
counted as a locally completed run.
