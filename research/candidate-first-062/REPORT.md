## Completeness and identification

Ciphertext determines every selected slot without knowing the key. Subtracting the emission-index key reconstructs plaintext; re-encryption follows the same route by induction on emitted symbols. Each clue therefore imposes exactly one key-residue equality. Conflicting equalities mean no key; otherwise unspecified digits are independent. Symbolic zero-vector removal subtracts one key only if zero belongs to the cylinder. Nominal-period overlaps are not summed into a fictitious union cardinality.

The independent linked-circle reference and production's sorted-list implementation pass 51,012 tiny forward/inverse/route cases and 365,616 complete cylinder checks against forward truth. Separate direct synthetic controls cover every one of 3,104 full-size settings. The zero-excision adapter passes all 340 tiny digit/null patterns and synthetic wrapper agreement.
