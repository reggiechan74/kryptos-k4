Fold, align, then pull one column at a time

Unmeasured flat-grid illustration using the existing modeled square-aperture font. It does not simulate the sculpture’s surveyed glyphs, curvature, thickness, illumination or actual foldability.

The initial tableau view is mirrored; the fold reflects it again. The glyphs below face the same way. At zero, the observed anchor is MYVGT opposite AABCD in column 2; no row shift is applied.

White squares show the selected view: common open area or an individual layer. Counters always describe the common aperture. Green borders mark identical A–Z labels. Gray cells are outside the two-sheet overlap and excluded from its denominator. Blank/padding cells are solid copper. Different glyphs can share openings. No partial aperture fragment is interpreted as a new letter.
