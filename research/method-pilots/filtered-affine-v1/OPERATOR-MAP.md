## 4. Unknown-alphabet causal linear chains: exact theorem to audit

### Comparator grammar

Let A,B:Z26->Z26 be any fixed bijections, epsilon_C,epsilon_P in {+1,-1}, and all j in a finite set J be positive. Define

    kappa_i = epsilon_C B(C_i) + epsilon_P A(P_i),
    kappa_i = d_i + sum_j [a_j kappa_(i-j)
                         + b_j A(P_(i-j)) + c_j B(C_(i-j))].

Coefficients are fixed in Z26. d_i may be any fixed position-only sequence. This supergrammar contains Scott's independently selected PT/CT alphabets and his drift/constant, ActiveTK E220's shared arbitrary S with three K/P/C lags, E224's single CT lag plus periodic offset, and E226's fixed-alphabet linear KEY recurrence. It does not contain arbitrary nonlinear lookup feedback, alphabets changing with state, negative lags, current-plaintext feedback on the recurrence RHS, or input-dependent coefficients.

### Reduction, including old plaintext feedback

Assume a fixed member of this grammar is exactly our complete causal operator at all interior positions. Put I=im(F), choose f0 in I, and hold the past fixed while current c varies. The old recurrence makes kappa_i independent of c, so

    epsilon_C B(c) + epsilon_P A(c-f) = r(f)
    for every c in Z26 and f in I.

The same f always gives the same r(f), because the displayed LHS is identical regardless of which history produced f. Subtract the equation for f0. For each d=f-f0, translations of A by d have a constant difference. The property closes under addition/inverse; therefore on H=<I-I> there is a homomorphism D:H->Z26 with A(t+d)-A(t)=D(d). Injectivity follows from bijectivity of A: D(d)=0 would force d=0. The cyclic subgroup H has order 1,2,13 or26. Every such injective homomorphism is multiplication by a unit u of Z26 on H (choose the nonzero mod13 multiplier and odd parity by CRT; order2 necessarily maps13 to13). Hence

    r(f)=v+w f on I,  w=-epsilon_P*u a unit.

Thus kappa_i=v+w F_i for every history. Eliminate old PT terms using

    A(P_(i-j)) = epsilon_P*kappa_(i-j)
                - epsilon_P*epsilon_C*B(C_(i-j)).

Substitution and division by unit w imply

    F_i = q0(i) + sum_j q_j F_(i-j) + sum_j t_j B(C_(i-j)).   (* )

This reduction does not assume A/B are affine on ALL of Z26, that im(F) contains a unit, or that its differences generate Z26. Only their translation action on H is used.

### Equality interaction cannot satisfy (* )

Choose p in {2,13} with a nonzero equality coefficient e in F mod p; choose the smallest starting lag m among its nonzero equality pairs (m=1,3,5). Every delayed nonzero equality pair starts at m'+j>m, because j>0 and m'>=m. Thus none is the pair (m,m+1).

At a sufficiently interior i, hold all ciphertext variables other than z=C_(i-m), w=C_(i-m-1) fixed. Every equality involving at most one of z,w becomes a unary function. Every quadratic/product contribution involving both variables is a scalar multiple of (z mod p)(w mod p); all other polynomial and B contributions are unary. Equation (* ) would imply, for ALL 26^2 symbol pairs,

    e*1[z=w] + a*(z mod p)*(w mod p) + f(z) + g(w) = 0.

The matrix e*I_26 has rank26 over F_p. The remaining three terms are a sum of at most three rank-one matrices. They cannot equal it. This contradicts e!=0 and proves E is globally disjoint from the entire stated chain grammar. Products, arbitrary unary alphabet relabelings and old plaintext/key feedback have explicitly been accounted for.
