## What was checked

The preflight counts the fixed reference traversal exactly:

```
W = sum_T consistency(T) * compatible_beta_count(T) * 13**(3 - rank(T))
```

Each original support is retained separately. Fresh raw F13 certificates undergo
the generator's replay and a second replay of serialized proof bytes. The
independent streaming checker rebuilds features and beta constraints, owns its
support cursor, running sum and content hash, and rejects incomplete, reordered
or altered evidence. No solver cache, support/beta merge, semantic pruning or
saturation is used.

Before execution, an explicit amendment separated generation and audit counters:
35,264 beta candidates and 846,336 parity-row tests per pass, or 70,528 and
1,692,672 combined. Both passes share the unchanged 50 million work / 20-second
preflight limits. The original proposal and amendment are retained separately.

For admitted B, the preflight did not replace the unchanged full verifier.
Mutual base inclusion, direct residual/cut comparisons, complete original
support/beta inventory, lifts, joints, masks, witnesses and provenance remained

The roles share the orchestration/generator and unchanged semantic engine.

roughly 0.1 seconds in these disclosed runs. This is a measured overhead lesson,
not a comparative performance campaign or permission to weaken the checks.
