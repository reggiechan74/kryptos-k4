# Crossed-alphabet adjacent affine digraphs

23 October 2026. All four missing crossed-coordinate structures are inconsistent within EASTNORTHEAST. No Berlin letters were used to fit or reject these structures. The phrase is nevertheless public and has been used elsewhere, not historically untouched data.

The older [parity boundary](AFFINE-DIGRAPH-PARITY-BOUNDARY.md) covered only equal input/output alphabets. This audit-driven extension freezes AZ→KRYPTOS and KRYPTOS→AZ at adjacent-pair origins 1 and 2. Each output coordinate has three arbitrary coefficients, solved exactly modulo2 and modulo13 with CRT. Noninvertible matrices are allowed as a necessary-condition relaxation; inconsistency therefore rejects every invertible matrix as well. It does not reject changing matrices, reordered/nonadjacent pairs, extra layers, or general nonlinear blocks.

Each first-crib system contains six complete known pairs. All four structures have zero compatible coefficient tuples. Exact row-combination contradiction certificates, ranks and equations are retained in [the result](../results/AFFINE-DIGRAPH-CROSSED-001.json). No enumeration of 26^6 matrices/offsets was necessary. The [pre-evaluation protocol](AFFINE-DIGRAPH-CROSSED-SPEC.md) and [controls](../results/AFFINE-DIGRAPH-CROSSED-001-controls.json) remain separate: sixteen literal-crib plants include invertible, singular and zero matrices; all true parameters are retained in the relaxed spaces. Thirty-six complete small-ring comparisons agree with direct three-variable enumeration. This closes four precise gaps, not the whole polygraphic family.
