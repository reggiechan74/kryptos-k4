# Neptunbrunnen–Weltzeituhr corridor: bounded period-map verification

Research date: 18 October 2026. This preserves a supplied research report.
Statements about visual inspection belong to that report; this coding update
did not retrieve or inspect the map images independently. One bounded pass over
a genuine pre-1989 map precedes any German-initial extraction. Geography is not
a K4 solution or evidence of an intended Kryptos link.

## Period evidence and limits

The researcher retrieved and visually inspected official Landesarchiv Berlin
HistoMap K5 sheet 423D, 1985, WMS layer `423_D_1985`. The K5 catalogue lists
editions 1966, 1970, 1977, 1984, 1985, 1993, 2012; it has no 1989 edition.
Adjacent K4 sheet 4231 lists 1981, 1984, 1991; those images were not needed or
inspected. The 1985 date comes from the catalogue; inspected crops omit marginal
dating. Changes between 1985 and 1989 remain outside this verification.

The map supports the Neptunbrunnen–Rathausstraße–railway-crossing–Alexanderplatz/
Weltzeituhr corridor and two flanking buildings. It prescribes neither a walk,
ten landmarks, an ordering, nor a cipher instruction.

| Fernsehturm | Printed footprint; smaller technical text was not used |

| Circular fountain symbol north/northwest of clock | No proper name in inspected crop; do not import a modern name |

Open landscaped space, a connected Rathausstraße sidewalk/street, the railway
underpass and the clock between blocks support a plausible mid-1980s approach.
Vehicles are drawn on Rathausstraße. This is not proof of a pedestrian-only or
unobstructed 1989 route, or of the exact current 532 m walking route.

The flanking geometry can support a modern architectural description of a
gateway. No printed gateway/Tor name was found in the inspected map. The user's
gateway wordplay remains interpretation, not a period-map label or cipher clue.

## Keep the geographic models separate

1. **Clock-terminal model:** start west-southwest near Neptunbrunnen and approach
   the clock east-northeast. Approximate center-to-center geometry is recorded in
   [the earlier note](world-clock-ene-geography.md).
2. **Geometric-midpoint model:** use the fixed reflected point
   **52.52273224799, 13.41976896153**. It is a separate geometric construction;
   do not snap it to a landmark. The supplied crop centered at rounded
   52.52273225, 13.41976896 places it in/along access-road/parking space near
   Parkplatz and Berolinastraße, with Haus der Statistik to the west. No distinct
   named monument was identified at that exact point. A georeferenced raster is
   not a survey.
3. **Walking-midpoint claim:** supplied current route halves are 532 m and
   622 m. They differ by 90 m, so the clock is not the midpoint of that walking
   route. Walking distance and the geometric construction are separate models.

German-initial extraction remains uninstantiated. A route, eligible named
features, historical naming, ordering and tie rules are not uniquely fixed.
Do not force a list of ten or choose initials to fit a desired key.
