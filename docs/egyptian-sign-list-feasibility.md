# Egyptian sign-list hypothesis: evidence and missing model

18 October 2026. Documentation of the user's hieroglyphics question and supplied
research findings only. No new source retrieval, mapping or cipher test was run.

There is a genuine Egyptian connection through K3's Howard Carter text and the
reported 2025 Egypt-trip clue. Supplied sources:
[CIA Kryptos document](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/DOC_0006122524.pdf)
and [Scientific American final-clue report](https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/cia-kryptos-puzzle-creator-releases-final-clues/).
This contextual connection does not prove a hieroglyphic mechanism, identify a
sign list, or supply a K4 key.

The supplied review of [The Met's Art of Ancient Egypt guide](https://www.metmuseum.org/-/media/files/learn/for-educators/publications-for-educators/the-art-of-ancient-egypt.pdf),
printed pages 47–48, describes approximately 24 single-consonant signs,
bi-/triliteral signs, word signs and silent determinatives, omission of vowels,
and reading orientation. This is not an interchangeable 26-letter English
alphabet. Sign function, direction and context matter; a lossy transcription
does not automatically give a reversible English encoding.

[Gardiner's 1927 grammar/sign-list context](https://egyptartefacts.griffith.ox.ac.uk/people/alan-henderson-gardiner/)
establishes historical availability as a possibility, not evidence that Sanborn
or Scheidt used it. No artist connection is established by that date alone.

H-EGYPTIAN-SIGN-LIST remains unspecified. Before a reproducible experiment, fix
the exact edition, selected signs, sign-to-symbol mapping, reading direction,
normalization, treatment of vowels/determinatives and ambiguities, inverse rule
and cipher/key model. No invented 26-glyph alphabet, Unicode code-point arithmetic,
translation fitting or arbitrary sign selection is authorized by this note.

A simple fixed one-to-one glyph renaming alone is structurally a monoalphabetic
substitution and inherits the already demonstrated monoalphabetic contradiction
at unchanged alignment. It does not evade the
[fixed-baseline structural constraints](../research/STRUCTURAL-FINDINGS.md).
Layered models with additional transformations are not excluded by that narrow
observation; they remain uninstantiated. No K4 solution or family-wide exclusion
is claimed. Source descriptions above are attributed to the supplied research;
the coding executor did not independently inspect the linked PDFs in this update.
