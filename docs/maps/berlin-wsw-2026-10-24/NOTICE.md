## UNESCO Sites Navigator boundary caveat
The distinct ArcGIS service prd_whc_sites_dossiers_elements_v2_view (item 0eb76eed88bf40a3bb76018fbd5f1fce) has blank license/copyright fields in retained service/layer metadata. No explicit redistribution license was verified. UNESCO's Terms of Use (updated 2026-09-15), sections 4–5, distinguish specifically licensed data, reserve substantial/systematic database reuse rights, and permit occasional individual non-commercial educational/research reproduction with attribution: https://www.unesco.org/en/terms-use . The technical note confirms State Party consent to display on Navigator, not a general downstream redistribution grant: https://whc.unesco.org/document/208934 . Do not label this service CC BY-SA based on the separate DataHub dataset.

For the lean repository bundle, omit full raw Navigator polygon dumps and combined copies containing them; retain source/query URLs, hashes, coverage counts, scripts, map outputs and analytical findings. Record that raw Navigator reconstruction requires applicable permission/terms to be checked. Do not represent this note as clearance for unrestricted public redistribution of all maps or datasets. Private preservation and public release are different uses. No determination is made here that these terms prohibit every independently generated factual map, or that they authorize unrestricted public distribution of this atlas. Applicability to these analytical map outputs remains unresolved; assess before public release. Do not hide this unresolved point by declaring the entire package MIT, CC0, or CC BY-SA.

## Packaging caution
OSM and Paris ODbL derivative portions should remain clearly identifiable and retain required notices. Avoid conflating their licenses with DataHub's CC BY-SA or other layers. The computational route is newly generated; the fact that it was clipped against a third-party boundary does not grant rights to redistribute that full boundary. Preserve source disclaimers and distinguish analysis outputs from authoritative data. This is a source-audit record, not legal advice.
