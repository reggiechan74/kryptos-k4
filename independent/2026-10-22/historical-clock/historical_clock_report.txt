ALEXANDERPLATZ WORLD CLOCK: PRE-1990 EVIDENCE PILOT
Prepared 22 October 2026. Scope: historical evidence for a Kryptos K4 hypothesis, not a claimed decoding.

RESULT
41 directly read city-label observations on 10 physical panels, in three disconnected dated photo segments. Literal spelling, physical band and within-panel label order are preserved. This is NOT a complete 1989 inventory. No photograph here establishes the full clock state in 1989.

A defensible reversible mechanical mapping is available at sector level: h = (s + phi) mod 24, with one global phase phi for the entire ring. Its inverse is s = (h - phi) mod 24. Keep the physical band unchanged if it is part of the coordinate. Represent dial 24 as residue 0. This is a constrained model of the clock, not an established A–Z cipher. The observations do not identify a ciphertext-to-city or ciphertext-to-coordinate encoder.

3. WHAT THE SOURCES CONSTRAIN, AND WHAT THEY DO NOT
The constructor describes two city rings with 24 panels each, a numbered middle ring rotating once per day, and a simplified planetary ornament rotating as a whole once per minute. Thus 24-sector order and a single shared moving phase have a physical basis. Actual planetary orbital periods do not have a basis in that mechanism. The constructor's general hemisphere description must not override directly visible city placement.

The 12 December 1997 Berliner Zeitung restoration report says 20 city names were added and time-zone assignments were changed, specifically discussing Kyiv relative to Moscow. A 20 December 1997 taz/dpa report also mentions additions and updated names such as St. Petersburg. These support a historical-version barrier; they do NOT supply a complete original inventory or prove that everything stayed unchanged from 1969 to 1989.

Exact underdetermination:
  - The three photographic runs have no observed overlap in this pilot. Their global relative offsets are not established. No joined 24-sector ring is supplied.
  - A photograph supplies an exposure-specific moving phase, not Sanborn's intended phase, epoch, traversal, or start point.
  - The source images do not define how 26 letters map to 24 sectors, or to multiple labels in the same sector.
  - Several city labels share a sector and band. Selecting a city per letter would add unobserved free choices.
  - A physical band is a spatial category, not a validated hemisphere bit, cipher bit, or A–M/N–Z divider.
  - City-label rank versus printed-line rank is a distinct choice when a city wraps across lines.
  - Exact German labels, accents, spacing, and historical names cannot be silently normalized into modern English forms.

A fully reversible label lookup exists only when the observation's source/segment, sector, physical band and label rank are retained. Collapsing the 41 labels to sector-plus-band produces only 17 distinct cells; collapsing them to sector produces 10. The lost distinctions cannot be recovered by a bare sector shift.

4. EXECUTED PILOT
Run after extraction: python historical_clock/pilot.py
Results in pilot_results.json:
  - 41 observation-identity/label round trips passed.
  - 1,152 mechanical slot round trips passed: 24 integer phases x 24 sectors x 2 physical bands.
  - Three segment-local numeral-order consistency checks passed.
  - Dataset checks enforce no admitted 1989 verification and no invented civil UTC offsets.
  - K4 trials executed: 0. There is no observation-grounded A–Z encoder to test yet.

The round-trip tests establish implementation consistency, not historical truth and not evidence that K4 uses this mechanism. Fractional phase is allowed physically; the integer checks are deliberately limited software sanity tests.

5. PRECISE NEXT PILOT AND STOP CONDITIONS
Evidence reconstruction:
  1. Obtain dated pre-1990 photographs or original drawings that overlap an admitted segment in at least two readable panels. Require visible seams, literal spelling, upper/lower assignment and sufficient numeral context.
  2. Have a second reader independently transcribe the candidate patch before consulting a modern label list. Resolve disagreements at the pixel/source level; leave unresolved cells unknown.
  3. Register only matching panel sequences, preserving separate historical versions when labels or placement differ. A 1969/1970/1984 collage must not be labeled a 1989 inventory.
  4. Promote an actual 1989 candidate only after contemporaneous coverage or documented unchanged state supports every consumed label/position.

Cryptographic pilot, only after an independently motivated encoder is supplied:
  - Freeze the historical version, spelling normalization, direction, start, band/row rule and alphabet conversion before scoring K4.
  - Use one global phase, not one phase per character. If phase is unknown, enumerate the explicitly declared global phase set once.
  - Require a total deterministic encoder and inverse and exact round-trip tests for every symbol consumed.
  - Do not use missing labels as free variables. Reject or suspend a candidate when it encounters an unsupported cell.
  - Evaluate a predeclared held-out crib/region and compare against matched null controls. A model chosen to fit one known word must earn support elsewhere.

Stop/suspend conditions:
  - Stop at this partial evidence artifact when the next claim requires missing panel registration, unverified 1989 continuity, unreadable text, or an invented letter/city selector.
  - Reject models requiring modern replacements, arbitrary per-letter city choices, independent per-character phases, actual planetary orbital periods, or a hemisphere-bit assumption.
  - If different admissible source readings change the candidate key, report an unresolved branch; do not choose whichever yields more English.
  - No decoded-message claim is warranted from this pilot.
