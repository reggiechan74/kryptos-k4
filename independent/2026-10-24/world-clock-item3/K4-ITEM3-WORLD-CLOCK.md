# Item 3: the historical Alexanderplatz World Clock

Research completed 24 October 2026 UTC. Target: an authentic 1989 city-panel/time mapping relevant to H-CLOCK-HISTORICAL and H-CLOCK-FACE. This is historical evidence reconstruction, not a K4 solution or a cryptanalytic test.

## Result

**A complete, independently fixed 1989 city/time-to-key mapping still cannot be specified. However, this pass found material new evidence, including direct evidence against treating all pre-1990 photographs as one unchanged inventory.**

1. A museum-catalogued **1971** photograph places **LONDON** and **ALGIER** differently from the admitted **3 October 1969** source. A separate Feist photograph labelled 1971 corroborates the later arrangement.
2. A photographer-dated **January 1974** photograph places **NEW YORK on the same upper panel as MONTREAL, WASHINGTON and HAVANNA**, unlike the admitted January 1970 photograph, where it occupies the next panel.
3. A larger, source-native version of the previously identified **May 1989** photograph supports **five complete city labels**, a six-face partial lower-band sequence, and a separate date-line inscription. This is genuine target-year evidence, but not a complete drum.
4. A new conservator's source shows an original removed inscription plate. An official university publication identifies the associated conservation thesis precisely: **Alexander Strauß, 2006, FH Potsdam**. Its full text and plate provenance remain unavailable in this pass.
5. A readable **3 October 1990** archival photograph supplies a useful Asia comparison and literal offset marks. It is explicitly post-1989 and cannot silently fill 1989 gaps.

No global true-north registration, full 1989 alteration history, creator-selected epoch, traversal, or independent A–Z bridge was recovered. **K4 trials executed: zero.** The conditional registration analysis being developed separately must remain conditional; these observations do not validate an unchanged-inventory assumption.

## Baseline and method

First reviewed the repository's H-CLOCK-HISTORICAL/H-CLOCK-FACE entries and historical-clock report/data, plus the saved historical-model, north-orientation and navigation dossiers. The baseline has 41 literal labels in ten panels across three dated segments: EU1969, AM1970 and AS1984. It is not a 1989 inventory. The museum model is already known to differ from installed panels; it was not reused as a substitute. The modern 146/148-city counts were not used.

New source discovery used Exa and Firecrawl, focusing on museum photo catalogues, the Deutsche Kinemathek archive, original conservation material and historical civil-time sources. Public source-native images were downloaded for private inspection. A second model reader independently transcribed the new images without modern city lists or the prior transcriptions. This reduces one-reader error but is **not** independent human expert certification. Source dates were verified separately against their catalogue pages. No AI enhancement or generative restoration was used. Images and crops are not embedded in this deliverable or included in the machine-readable inventory.

All panel identifiers below are local to a photograph and progress photo-left to photo-right. They are fixed face boundaries, not moving hour numerals, UTC offsets or compass directions. Null/missing entries mean unknown or untranscribed, never an empty historical panel. Partial words, diacritics, plus signs and line wrapping are not silently normalized.

### Discrepancy with the 1969 baseline

The exact baseline image was downloaded and visually rechecked; its SHA-256 matches the repository's stored hash. It shows:

- 1969: LONDON / LISSABON in the upper face immediately left of BERLIN's face; ALGIER / TUNIS in BERLIN's lower face.
- 1971: DUBLIN / LISSABON / ALGIER in that corresponding upper face; LONDON and TUNIS in BERLIN's lower face, together with KOPENHAGEN / WIEN / ROM / BELGRAD.

The adjoining AZOREN, REYKJAVIK, BERLIN and BUKAREST/HELSINKI/SOFIA sequence supports the local correspondence. This is not inferred from the numbered ring. The images demonstrate **different observed arrangements**, not merely differing clock phases. They do not identify the exact alteration date, cause, or subsequent 1989 state. In particular, a stronger claim that a specific maintenance operation moved specific plates has not been established.

## 6. Historical civil time is not the installed panel program

The new photographs make it important to separate three different things:

1. Physical city-label position and any printed correction mark.
2. The numbered ring's common mechanical phase at an exposure.
3. A city's legal civil time on a specified date, including daylight-saving rules.

The third cannot be substituted for the first to force a registration. Singapore supplies a particularly clear example. The October 1990 artifact still shows SINGAPUR+ in the Bangkok/Hanoi group and a separate +30 notation. Singapore's official Standard Time Notification, G.N. S 392/1981, instead specifies **GMT+8 from 16:00 GMT on 31 December 1981**. This is primary legal-history evidence, not an inference from a modern timezone website. It does not by itself tell us how the Berlin operator calibrated the ring.

- Official historical notification: https://sso.agc.gov.sg/SL-Rev/IA1965-N1/Published/20000831?DocDate=19920325
- Contemporary release catalogue, 20 December 1981, document 985-1981-12-20: https://www.nas.gov.sg/archivesonline/speeches/record-details/7b34212e-115d-11e3-83d5-0050568939ad

The currently published IANA source also records Britain’s permanent BST interval through 31 October 1971 and different 1989 autumn return dates for Britain versus the EU rules applied to Berlin. Its rules yield Berlin/London offsets of +2/+1 in May 1989, +1/+1 on 7 October, and +1/+0 on 9 November. These examples were also sanity-checked with local zoneinfo; they are illustrative civil-time comparisons, **not chosen Sanborn dates or historical clock readings**. IANA itself warns that historical entries are not infallible and often derive from secondary compilations.

IANA source: https://data.iana.org/time-zones/tzdb/europe

An invariant pair of city panels and one common ring phase preserve their displayed difference. They cannot automatically implement a civil-time difference that changes seasonally. Any proposed mechanism must therefore declare whether it means the literal installed display, standard-time conventions, or legally correct date-specific civil time. It must not switch interpretations when a crib fails.

## 7. What can and cannot now be specified

The maker-backed 24-sector/common-phase mechanism remains a legitimate mechanical abstraction: a fixed sector index plus a single common daily phase, modulo 24, with any source-attested corrections separately recorded. The old integer round-trip tests remain implementation checks, not historical proof. A physical fractional phase remains possible.

The new evidence strengthens these restrictions:

- **Historical version is a model input.** “Pre-1990” is not a single demonstrated layout. EU1969 versus D71 and AM1970 versus J74 now exhibit actual disagreements.
- **A shared city name does not justify erasing revision boundaries.** Locally corresponding panels can be described, but a collage cannot become a 1989 inventory without dated continuity evidence.
- **Do not collapse city identity into a panel.** Multiple cities share a face, so a city selector or rank rule remains necessary for an invertible label lookup.
- **Keep printed offset marks separate.** SINGAPUR+ and +30-like annotations show that a pure integer-sector-only representation may discard literal information.
- **No independently grounded alphabet bridge exists.** Neither these images nor the thesis citation tell us how 26 letters select 24 sectors, cities, rows or corrections.
- **No north registration was gained.** Visible background buildings and later geocoding are useful potential photogrammetric inputs, not a measured 15-degree face registration with tolerances.

A complete deterministic key would still require: a source-fixed 1989 inventory for every consumed cell; an independently selected origin and direction; a justified epoch/phase; a city/band/rank rule; and a total alphabet conversion. None should be fitted per letter or selected for English-looking output.
